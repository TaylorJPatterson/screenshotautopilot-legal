Where is the app?

Screenshot Autopilot lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. After launch, look for the viewfinder icon at the top right of the screen. Click it for Rename Now, Preview Renames, Pause, Undo Last Run, Rename History, Settings, and Quit.

Nothing is being renamed

Open Settings from the menu bar icon and check the folder is in the watch list. If a folder stopped working (for example after being moved), remove it and add it again — picking it in the folder chooser is what grants the app access. The app only renames files whose names match the macOS screenshot pattern, such as Screenshot 2026-08-11 at 12.03.49 PM.png.

Undoing a rename

Every rename is recorded. Use Undo Last Run in the menu bar for the most recent batch, or Rename History to undo any earlier rename, one file or a whole run. A file you have replaced since is left alone rather than overwritten.

Restoring your purchase

The App Store version renames 5 files free; a single one-time purchase, Unlimited Renaming, removes the limit. To restore it on a new Mac or after reinstalling, click the menu bar icon, choose Unlock Unlimited…, and click Restore Purchases. The unlock is tied to the Apple Account you bought it with.

Frequently asked

Does it need the internet? Renaming runs entirely on your Mac. Only the purchase and its restore talk to the App Store. There is no account and no subscription.

Does the app collect my data? No. See our Privacy Policy.

Why are names shorter on my Mac? On Macs without Apple Intelligence the app names files with its built-in reader, which produces shorter, plainer names. That is expected, not a fault.
