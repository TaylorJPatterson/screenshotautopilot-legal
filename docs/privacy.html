Privacy Policy

SmartRenamer · Last updated: August 28, 2026

SmartRenamer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your Mac. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The only network activity in the App Store version is Apple's own StoreKit system checking and processing the optional purchase; the app itself sends nothing anywhere. Renaming works the same with Wi-Fi off.

Your screenshots

SmartRenamer reads the screenshots in the folders you choose in order to give each file a descriptive name. All of that reading happens on your Mac, using Apple's on-device Vision, NaturalLanguage and, where available, Apple Intelligence frameworks. The images and the text read out of them never leave your Mac and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

The app keeps its settings, a log, and a rename history (so renames can be undone) in your user Library folder. These files stay on your Mac, are readable only by your user account, and are removed when you delete the app's data.

Purchases

The App Store version renames 5 files free, then offers a single one-time purchase that removes the limit. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
